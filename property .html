Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How many square feet are in 1 standard Marla?
A standard Marla equals 225 square feet in the traditional Pakistani measurement system. However, the international standard Marla is 272.25 square feet. Always verify your local standard before making property calculations, as some regions may use the alternative 250 sq ft standard.
❓ What is the difference between 225 sq ft and 272.25 sq ft Marla standards?
The 225 sq ft Marla is the traditional Pakistan standard used in most major cities like Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. The 272.25 sq ft Marla is based on metric conversion (approximately 25.29 sq meters) and is used in some international contexts. Choose the standard that matches your location's property documents to ensure accurate conversion.

Simply select your Marla standard, enter the Marla value, and click Convert. The tool instantly displays the Kanal equivalent (1 Kanal = 20 Marlas by standard conversion). This makes it easy to calculate larger land parcels measured in Kanals.
